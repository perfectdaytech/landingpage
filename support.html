Questions for all apps

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-in. Everything you enter stays on your device.
Is my data backed up or synced?
There's no cloud sync. Your data is stored on your device only (and in your phone's own iCloud/Android backup, if you've turned that on). Each app lets you make a free export file. Export and backup files are plain, unencrypted files, so keep them somewhere private.
Is the unlock a subscription?
No. Each app's unlock is one payment, not a subscription, and nothing renews. The price is shown in the app before you pay.
How does the free trial work?
Swipestart: every feature free for 21 days. Calmdays and Wool Tally: every feature free for 30 days. Bleep Test Pack UK: every feature free for 8 test runs or 90 days, whichever comes first. The free days are counted on your phone, so reinstalling or restoring a backup may not restart them. After the trial the core features stay free; see our Terms for the full list for each app.
I paid but the extras aren't on / I changed phone.
Open Settings and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. Check you have an internet connection for the store check. A backup file can't restore the unlock.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are too: reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple) or Google Play refund help. Your statutory rights are not affected.

Swipestart

What does the unlock include?
Calibrate insights (your multiplier and by-type split), your own templates and packs, wrap-up nudges and scheduled backup. [TO CONFIRM: final Swipestart unlock list]
How do I move to a new phone?
Make an export/backup file in Settings, send it to yourself (for example via Files, AirDrop or email), then import it in the app on the new phone. Then tap Restore purchase. Basic CSV export is free; the unlock adds a full backup file.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → Delete everything erases all app data on this device. Deleting the app also removes it.

Calmdays

What does the unlock include?
Creating a third and further counters. Two counters, milestones, the reminder, app lock, backup/export, help lines and delete-all stay free.
How do I move to a new phone?
Make an export/backup file in Settings, send it to yourself (for example via Files, AirDrop or email), then import it in the app on the new phone. Then tap Restore purchase. The file includes counter names and your reason, so keep it private.
Does Calmdays share anything about my habit?
No. There are no analytics or trackers, and nothing leaves your phone unless you export it. In discreet mode, reminders don't name your counter.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → Delete all my data erases everything on this device (counters, history, reminder, PIN). Deleting the app also removes it. Export files you saved elsewhere aren't affected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our apps and services.
