This policy covers these apps from PerfectDay Technologies:

  • Calmdays

1. Who we are

The apps are published by PerfectDay Technologies Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16952922), registered office: 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom ("PerfectDay", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for any personal data described in this policy. Contact: support@perfectdaytech.com (privacy questions: privacy@perfectdaytech.com).

ICO registration number: ZC144477

2. The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • What you enter stays on your device. We do not run servers for these apps and there is no cloud sync.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and no selling of data.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking tools are built into the apps.
  • Each app has a free trial and an optional one-off unlock. There are no subscriptions. Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), not by us.

3. What each app stores on your device

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it, access it or recover it. Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup or Android backup), if you have turned it on, may include the app's data; that backup is handled by Apple or Google under their terms.

Swipestart

  • Your tasks, time estimates, step ladders ("make it smaller" steps) and any templates you create.
  • Timed-task history (your guesses vs actual time), used to calculate your personal estimate multiplier.
  • Your 14-day show-up record and settings.
  • The date your free days began and whether you own the unlock.

Calmdays

  • Your counters: name, start date/time, optional money-per-day amount, reset history and badges/milestones.
  • An optional "reason" you write.
  • Settings (reminder time, discreet mode, show money, app lock).
  • If you turn on the app lock: a salted, one-way hash of your PIN (kept in the device's secure storage and excluded from backups). Face ID / fingerprint is handled entirely by your device; the app never receives biometric data.
  • The date your free days began and whether you own the unlock.

Because counters can reveal a habit you are changing (for example alcohol, smoking or gambling), this information may relate to your health. None of it leaves your device unless you choose to export it. Calmdays will never include analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools.

Wool Tally

  • Your projects, row/stitch/repeat counts, pattern notes and row alerts.
  • Progress photos you choose to add (copied into the app's private storage) [TO CONFIRM: photos are copied into the app, not just linked].
  • Settings and theme.
  • The date your free days began and whether you own the unlock.

Bleep Test Pack UK

  • Your results history (test type, level and shuttle reached, date, and an estimated VO2 max if shown).
  • Settings (timing chart, chosen voice, and an optional age field used only for display).
  • Your trial progress (runs used, date your free days began) and whether you own the unlock.

Fitness results can be personal information. They stay on your device. The app does not connect to Apple Health or Health Connect.

4. Information that leaves your device

  • Only when you choose: if you use export or backup, the app creates a file and opens your device's share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, email, a cloud drive) is your choice and is governed by that service. Backup files are plain, unencrypted files and may contain everything you've entered, so store them carefully.
  • To Apple or Google: when you buy or restore an unlock, the store processes that transaction under its own privacy policy.
  • To us: nothing, unless you email us.

App-specific points:

  • Swipestart – calendar: if you allow it, the app reads the time of your next calendar event on your device to show a countdown. Event details are not stored outside the app or sent anywhere. Wrap-up nudges are local notifications scheduled on your device; there is no push server.
  • Calmdays – help-line links: if you tap a help-line or support link, it opens in your browser or phone dialler. The app does not tell that organisation anything about you; their own privacy policy applies once you visit. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. There is no push server and no notification token is sent anywhere. In discreet mode the notification does not name your counter.
  • Bleep Test Pack UK – AI voice cues. The spoken cues in the app were generated in advance using an AI voice service (ElevenLabs) and are bundled in the app. When you use the app, it plays these stored files on your device. The app does not connect to ElevenLabs or any other voice service, and no personal data, recordings, test results or other information about you is sent to them. If a spoken cue uses your device's built-in text-to-speech, that is handled by your device's operating system [TO CONFIRM: whether Bleep uses device text-to-speech at all; some Android engines may use the network].

5. Permissions

  • Swipestart: Calendar (optional, read-only), asked for when you open the Time tab, to count down to your next event; Notifications (optional), for wrap-up nudges. No location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • Calmdays: Notifications (optional), for the daily reminder only; Face ID / biometrics (optional), to unlock the app if you turn on app lock. No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or health records (Apple Health / Health Connect).
  • Wool Tally: Photos (optional): you choose photos with your device's photo picker; the app only receives the photos you pick and does not get access to your whole library. Keep-awake can keep the screen on while you count; this needs no data. No camera, microphone, location or contacts.
  • Bleep Test Pack UK: Background audio (iPhone) / a media-playback notification (Android) so beeps continue with the screen locked during a test. No microphone, camera, location or contacts.

You can change permissions at any time in your device settings.

6. Purchases

The apps are free to download. All features are free for a trial period counted on your device, after which you can keep using the free features or unlock the rest with a one-off in-app purchase. There are no subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your card details, name or email address. The app checks with the store whether you own the unlock so it can switch extra features on and let you restore the purchase. We store the date your free days began on your device. We don't send it anywhere.

7. Analytics

The apps do not contain analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and do not send us information about how you use them. If that ever changes we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

8. Our legal basis (UK GDPR)

Because the data described above stays on your device and is not sent to us, we generally do not process it ourselves. Where we do process personal data (for example when you email us), we rely on our legitimate interests in answering you and supporting the app (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), or on taking steps to perform our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)).

9. Emailing us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to reply and to help with the app, and we keep it for up to 24 months [TO CONFIRM: 24-month retention for support emails] after the conversation ends, unless we need it longer for a legal reason. Please don't send us sensitive personal information you don't need to share.

10. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Swipestart: Settings → Delete everything erases all app data on this device.
  • Calmdays: Settings → Delete all my data erases your counters, history, reminder and PIN from this device.
  • Wool Tally: Settings → Delete all data removes projects, notes and photos from this device.
  • Bleep Test Pack UK: [TO CONFIRM: whether Bleep has an in-app delete option and what it is called]

Deleting an app also removes its data. We cannot delete export files you saved elsewhere, or copies in your device backup; delete those yourself.

11. Children

Calmdays and Bleep Test Pack UK are intended for adults (18+). Swipestart and Wool Tally are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because we hold no app data about you, most requests will relate to support emails. To exercise a right, email support@perfectdaytech.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

13. Changes to this policy

If the way an app handles data changes (for example, if we ever add a feature that sends data off your device), we will update this policy and the store privacy labels before that version is released, and change the "Last updated" date above.

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our applications, features used, and time spent

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Data Storage and Security

Local-First Architecture: Our applications are designed with a local-first approach. Your data is primarily stored on your device using secure local storage (IndexedDB). This means:

  • Your data remains on your device by default
  • The app works fully offline
  • You have full control over your data

Cloud Sync (Optional): If you choose to enable cloud backup or collaboration features, your data will be securely stored on our servers using industry-standard encryption.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Sharing

Your Rights and Choices

  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data

International Data Transfers
